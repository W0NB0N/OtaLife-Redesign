Ota Life app teardown

Ota Life is a gamified anime fan app for India: a reel feed, daily quizzes, gacha rewards, community voting, and a shop that sells merch and tickets for the Ōta TOKYO festival. This page maps every feature visible in the 16 screenshots, plus what public listings say, as the input for the redesign.

The store copy pitches the app as "all-in-one for anime fans, rooted in Japanese culture", connecting fans in India and Japan. It lists earning 5 pts per 5 seconds of video and 200 pts per correct quiz answer. The festival (Yashobhoomi, Delhi) claims a 15,000-attendee Bangalore edition and targets 50,000 in Delhi; the app is its digital companion and ticket channel.

UX issues in the current build

Observed directly in the screenshots. These are the problems the redesign should fix, before any visual style is chosen.

High
Five currencies, no explanationPoints, XP, gacha tickets, daily votes and streak each live on a different tab. Store copy says 200 pts per answer while the app shows 1,000 pts per challenge. Users can't tell what earns what.
High
Leaderboard has no tiebreakEvery visible player scores 1000, so rank order looks arbitrary. Time taken or streak should break ties.
High
Ticket tiers hidden in body textGeneral / Prime / VIP are described in a long paragraph, while the CTA shows a single Rs. 299 "Book Now". There's no tier selector before checkout.
High
Notification FAB collides with contentOn Voting the bell renders over every card and stacks over the "+" FAB. On Quiz it covers the Daily Quest list.
Medium
Vote goals are meaningless1,720 votes against a 100 goal; every bar is pinned at 100%, so progress tells you nothing.
Medium
Gacha dead end at 0 ticketsThe main screen of the Rewards tab is a disabled button. The earn hints are text, not actions that link to the quiz or the login reward.
Medium
Conflicting difficulty labels"…Beginner" and "…Advanced" quizzes are both tagged "Intermediate".
Medium
Tab names don't match screen titlesVoting opens "Ideas", Shop opens "Marketplace". The top bar repeats the same four icons on every tab.
Medium
Survey is a long formFive questions on one scroll, no progress, no skip, and T-shirt size asked before the user has seen the app.
Medium
Ranks and badges are illegibleTiny unlabeled badge icons; "B-E" rank code is unexplained; perks ("0% Cashback / No Points Usage") read as a penalty.
Low
Test data in productionA community named "Name / Test", a shop called "Test", blank placeholder covers in Featured Shops.
Low
Copy carried over from another templateAn artist poll is headed "Vote for Your Favorite Design"; the disabled vote CTA has low contrast; stray decorative shapes overlap the cards.
Low
Quiz feedback is unclearThe timer is always red, and there's no visible right/wrong reveal or reward moment after Submit.

Pointers for the gamified redesign

Style is still open. These follow from the structure, whatever the look ends up being.

One wallet, one level

  • Merge points and coins; show XP as a level bar on the avatar
  • Tickets and votes become "energy" items in the same wallet sheet
  • Every reward animates into the wallet

A home that is a quest board

  • Today's quests, streak flame, and gacha tickets on one screen
  • Each quest row is a button that jumps to the task

Use the mascots

  • The three splash characters (pink girl, hamster, husky) can guide onboarding, cheer quiz results, and fill empty states

Make the ninja ladder a map

  • Student → Genin → … as a visible path with perks at each stop
  • Badges get names and unlock conditions

Quiz as a game round

  • Instant right/wrong feedback, combo meter, results card with points, XP, ticket earned, and rank change
  • Leaderboard with time tiebreak and "you" pinned

The event as a season

  • Countdown to Ōta TOKYO, season pass progress, ticket in a wallet with QR
  • Votes and quizzes feed into event perks
