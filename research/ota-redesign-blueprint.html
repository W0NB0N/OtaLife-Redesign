Redesign · user flow & progression · v1

Ōta redesign blueprint

Three mascots run the app, and one power stat, Ōra, measures everything you do in it. This page fixes the rules: who owns what, how Ōra is earned and spent, the six ranks and their bosses, how rank shows up on your avatar, and the flows a demo needs to walk through. Visual style comes later.

The cast

Each mascot owns a domain, a colour and a tone of voice. Wherever their domain appears, they appear: as the screen host, in empty states, in reward moments and in notifications. All three show up together only for the biggest moments: first launch, rank-ups and legendary pulls.

Partner mascot

In onboarding you pick one mascot as your partner. Your partner greets you on Home, delivers your star-up toasts, lends a costume piece to your avatar, and lands the finishing blow in your boss fights. It has no effect on how much Ōra you earn, and you can switch partners any time from your profile.

Ōra: the one power stat

Ōra replaces points, coins, XP and the separate rank score. Everything you do earns Ōra, every rank is an Ōra threshold, and the shop and capsule machine take Ōra.

Rank ladder

Six ranks above the starting rank. Each rank has three stars at even steps of Power. A star-up is a quick toast from your partner. A rank-up is a boss fight cutscene. Onboarding is tuned so every new user reaches Kōhai, and sees their first boss, in the first session.

Boss cutscenes

A rank-up fires when Power crosses a threshold, wherever that happens. The cutscene runs full screen, lasts about 8 seconds, and can be skipped after the first beat. Every boss you beat is kept in your journey map on Me, where you can replay its fight.

0.0 s · Lock-in

Ōra overload

The Ōra bar overflows and the screen cracks into a battle arena.

1.5 s · Boss intro

Gate boss appears

Name card slams in ("SPOILER KAPPA blocks the way to Kōhai!").

3.0 s · Team-up

Mascots attack

Puri leads, TAKU and Momo join with their signature moves.

5.0 s · Finisher

Partner + you

Your avatar and partner fire the Ōra beam. Boss poofs into sparkles.

6.5 s · Spoils

Reward reveal

New frame snaps onto your avatar, new title, perks list, ticket drop. "Share" and "Continue".

Rules

  • The fight always ends in a win. It's a celebration, not a test.
  • The cutscene waits if you're mid-quiz or mid-checkout, and plays at the next natural pause.
  • Your partner gets the finishing blow and a line written for that boss.
  • Reduced-motion setting swaps it for a still comic-panel sequence.

Star-ups (no boss)

  • Partner pops in from the screen edge with a one-line cheer
  • Ōra chip ring refills, star count bumps
  • +1 Standard ticket
  • Lasts 2 seconds and never blocks input

Daily systems

Quest board Home

  • 3 daily quests, one from each mascot (e.g. TAKU: watch 2 reels · Puri: play the Daily Challenge · Momo: check in)
  • Every quest row is a button that takes you straight to the task
  • Clearing all three: +150 Ōra and a Standard ticket
  • 7 weekly quests, a bigger reward and a Rare+ ticket

Streak Momo

  • Kept by finishing the Daily Challenge
  • Flame grows at 3, 7, 30 and 100 days and raises the Ōra multiplier
  • Encore shields cover one missed day each (earned at 7 days, or 500 Ōra, hold 2)
  • At 8 pm Momo sends one nudge if the streak is at risk. That's the only nudge of the day.

Cheers TAKU

  • Daily votes for the Fan Council, renamed Cheers
  • 3 a day, rising to 6 at Kami
  • Shown as glowsticks on the Fan Council screen
  • Unused Cheers expire at midnight

Battles & leaderboards Puri

  • Ties broken by total answer time, then by who finished first
  • Your own row is always pinned at the bottom of the list
  • Weekly Arena: brackets of 30 players near your rank, top 3 rewarded on Sunday night
  • Difficulty is one consistent label: Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced

Momo's capsule machine

Three ticket tiers stay, with odds always shown next to the Pull button. With no tickets, the machine offers a pull for Ōra and links to the next quest that drops a ticket.

Prize pool

  • Avatar parts and stickers (Common–Epic)
  • Mascot trading cards: digital, with a Legendary holo set
  • Shop coupons (5–20% off) that apply automatically at checkout
  • Event perks: fast-lane pass, sushi workshop seat, meet-and-greet raffle entry (Legendary)

Rules

  • Duplicates convert back to Ōra (20 / 60 / 150 / 500 by rarity)
  • No real-money pulls; tickets come only from play or Ōra
  • Capsule colour hints at the rarity before it opens
  • Prizes go to the Cabinet, sorted by expiry with a "use now" button

Fan Council (voting)

Ideas move through stages with real goals, so the progress bar always shows the next step and never sits at 100%.

1 · Spark100 Cheers in 14 daysNew idea, visible to everyone; TAKU checks it for duplicates and content rules

Polls (like "which artist should we invite?") are a separate card type: fixed options, a deadline, one Cheer each, and results shown after you vote.

Demo script

One continuous path, about 6 minutes, that shows every system. Seed the demo account so step 6 crosses into Senpai.

  1. Launch: mascot splash, Momo's welcome cards, pick Puri as partner.
  2. Mascot chats: pick Darling in the FranXX and Jujutsu Kaisen for TAKU, Mobile for Puri, Meeting voice actors for Momo. Ōra ticks up after each.
  3. Avatar: build a chibi, Puri lends a headset.
  4. Warm-up battle: answer the ダーリン question, see the combo and TAKU's note on a wrong answer.
  5. First boss: Spoiler Kappa cutscene → Kōhai, sakura frame. Momo hands over a Standard ticket.
  6. First pull: guided pull reveals a Rare sticker. (Switch to the seeded account at 2,900 Power.) Clear the daily quest board; the +150 crosses 3,000 and Filler Tanuki falls → Senpai.
  7. Fan Council: cheer the DARUMA Rollin' poll, watch TAKU's glowsticks, see an idea move from Spark to Hype.
  8. Stage: open Ōta TOKYO Delhi, pick Prime, see the auto-applied coupon and the Ōra slider capped at 10%, pay, get the QR ticket from Momo.
  9. Social proof: open a comment thread and see frames and titles; tap a frame for the rank card.
  10. Me: journey map with two bosses defeated, replay one fight.
